## Needs expert review
1. MSA vs marketing claims: the writer brief and several pages (Sydney custom FAQ, How we work sections) say "code in your repository from day one" and "IP transfers on completion/payment". MSA 10.6 and 7.4 let us hold the repository during development and withhold final repository access until payment. The pages have been softened to "our practice", but Andy or Square Legal should decide whether the MSA or the copy should change.
2. The MSA liability cap (18.2) is one-way and 18.4 excludes all indemnities. The checklist recommends a mutual cap and an IP non-infringement indemnity as "what good looks like", which our own MSA does not offer. That is not a factual error, but a buyer comparing the two may notice.
3. MSA 17.3 relies on ACL s 64A to limit consumer-guarantee remedies for non-household services. The checklist says a contract "generally can't take those rights away". That is accurate, but a lawyer may want to mention s 64A limitation.
4. VPDSS scope: the text now says Part 4 of the PDP Act. Whether Part 5 (law enforcement data security) bodies should also be mentioned needs a check against the current PDP Act.
5. HRIP private sector turnover wording follows the IPC's summary. The statutory test (HRIP s 11 and the definition of private sector person) is more nuanced.
