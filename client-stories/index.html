Scott

Runs an online marketplace

The payments audit that saved a marketplace before it launched.

The situation
Scott's marketplace takes payments from customers and passes the money on to the people providing the service. Everything seemed to be working, so he had not given the payment side much thought.
Pain points
  • Everything seemed to be working, so the payment side had not been given much thought.
  • How the platform handled other people's money could have led the payment provider to freeze the account.
  • There was a licensing and classification issue the business did not know existed.
How we got there
  1. We insisted on properly auditing the setup before building anything further.
  2. We found the money handling issue and the licensing and classification issue.
  3. We restructured how the money moves through the platform.
  4. It was all caught before the marketplace properly launched.
The result
The problems were caught before the marketplace properly launched, and everything is now set up the way it should be.

That one issue could have brought the whole marketplace to a halt.

Scott
Read the full transcript

I run an online marketplace so we're taking payments from customers and then passing that money on to people providing the service. I genuinely thought we had the payment side sorted. Everything seemed to be working, so honestly I hadn't really given it much thought. I went with Andy and the team because they insisted on properly auditing our setup before we went any further. And thank goodness they did. They found an issue with how we were handling other people's money that could have resulted in our payment provider freezing their account. There was also a licensing and classification issue I didn't even know existed. They called it before we properly launched and restructured how the money moves. So now everything's set up the way it should be. Was it worth it? Absolutely. That one issue could have brought the whole marketplace to a halt. If you're taking payments, I'd definitely get your setup checked before you start scaling.
