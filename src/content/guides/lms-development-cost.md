## Should you buy, host or build an LMS?

**Buy a SaaS LMS if you are training your own staff; host Moodle if you want control without a build; build custom only when learning is your product or must live inside your own platform.** That one decision moves the budget more than any feature list.

| Path | What you pay for | Best fit | Where it hurts |
|---|---|---|---|
| SaaS LMS (TalentLMS, LearnWorlds, LearnUpon, Docebo) | A monthly or yearly subscription, usually per active user or per enrolment | Employee training, compliance courses, small course businesses | Per user costs at scale; limited control of data model and reporting |
| Open source (Moodle, Totara) | Hosting, a partner for setup and plugins, upgrades and support | Education providers, associations, organisations wanting control | Upgrades and plugin compatibility are your problem; user experience needs work |
| Custom LMS | Design and build, then hosting and maintenance | Course businesses where learning is the product; LMS features inside an existing app or portal | Highest upfront cost; you own the roadmap and the maintenance |

Most Australian employers should not build an LMS. The SaaS market is mature, prices are published, and the hard parts (content players, completion tracking, reminders) are solved. Our [build vs buy guide](/guides/build-vs-buy-software) sets out the general test.

## What do SaaS LMS platforms charge, and when does it get expensive?

**SaaS LMS pricing is low for a stable, known audience and climbs quickly when the audience is large, occasional or outside your organisation.** Published prices at the time of writing (November 2026):

| Vendor | Pricing model | Published price |
|---|---|---|
| TalentLMS | Monthly active users, billed yearly or monthly, USD | Core from US$119 a month; Grow US$229; Pro US$449, with a per user rate above the plan limit |
| LearnWorlds | Plan fee, plus an enrolment fee on the entry plan, USD | Starter 49 a month (44 on annual billing) plus $5 per course enrolment; Pro Trainer 99 (79 annual); Learning Center 299 (249 annual) |
| LearnUpon | Quote based, plans quoted from 100, 150 or 300 users | Not published; says it is not a fit below 100 users |
| MoodleCloud | Hosted Moodle in tiers by user count | Own domain is an extra $160 AUD a year on the Medium plan; plugins cannot be installed |

The pattern to watch is the audience. An employer with 200 staff who all log in most months pays a predictable fee. An association with 8,000 members, of whom 1,500 take one course a year, either pays for capacity it rarely uses or pays per enrolment on top. A course business selling 2,000 enrolments a year on an entry plan pays $10,000 in enrolment fees alone, which is why LearnWorlds nudges growing sellers to a higher tier.

Currency matters too. Most of these vendors bill in US dollars, so your cost moves with the exchange rate, and GST may be charged on top as an imported service.

## What does hosted Moodle cost to run?

**Moodle itself has no licence fee because it is open source; the cost is hosting, configuration, plugins and the people who keep it upgraded.** For organisations that want ownership of their learning data without commissioning a build, it is often the middle path.

Expect three cost lines. First, setup: branding, course structure, roles, single sign on and a few plugins, typically done by a Moodle partner. Second, hosting and support: either MoodleCloud for small sites (with no plugins allowed) or a partner hosted site with a support agreement. Third, upgrades: Moodle releases major versions regularly, and every plugin you add must be checked against each one. That last line is where Moodle budgets drift.

As planning figures, not quotes, a partner configured Moodle with branding and two or three integrations is often scoped in the $10k to $40k range, with hosting and support at $3k to $15k a year depending on users and service level. Get two partner quotes before you rely on either number.

## What does a custom LMS cost to build?

**Worked at an assumed agency rate of $1,100 to $1,500 a day, a custom LMS ranges from about $60k for a focused course platform to $400k or more for an enterprise system.** The rate assumption reflects Re:Sourced's July 2026 guide, which puts senior software contractors in Sydney at $800 to $1,100 a day ex GST; agencies charge more to cover design, testing and project management. Conduct's 2026 guide puts educational software at roughly $15k for basic tools, $80k for medium complexity and $150k or more for advanced platforms, which is consistent with these bands.

| Build | Typical scope | Effort | Cost range (AUD, ex GST) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Focused course platform | Course catalogue, video lessons, quizzes, progress, Stripe checkout, certificates | 55 to 75 days | $60k to $110k |
| Course platform with CPD | Above, plus CPD points by category, annual statements, member pricing, admin reporting | 75 to 100 days | $85k to $150k |
| Enterprise LMS | Single sign on with Entra ID, HR system sync, compliance assignments and reminders, manager dashboards, audit trail, AI tutoring | 140 to 280+ days | $150k to $400k+ |

### Worked example: a professional association's CPD platform

This is a hypothetical scope to show how the effort adds up. An association with 6,000 members wants members to buy short courses, complete them online and receive a CPD statement that matches its board's categories.

| Work package | Effort |
|---|---|
| Discovery, information architecture and design | 9 days |
| Member login linked to the existing membership system | 6 days |
| Course builder for staff: modules, video, documents, quizzes | 14 days |
| Learner progress, completion rules and certificates (PDF) | 9 days |
| CPD rules engine: points by category, caps, annual statements | 10 days |
| Checkout with member and non member pricing, GST invoices, Xero sync | 9 days |
| SCORM 1.2 player for purchased content | 6 days |
| Admin reporting and exports | 6 days |
| Testing, accessibility review, launch and training | 11 days |
| **Total** | **80 days** |

At $1,100 a day that is $88,000; at $1,500 a day, $120,000, ex GST. Dropping SCORM and the Xero sync would save about 10 days. Adding a native mobile app would add more than either.

## What will each path cost over three years?

**Over three years, SaaS usually wins for small and medium audiences, and custom only pulls ahead when per user fees are large or the platform earns revenue.** These totals use stated assumptions, not quotes: SaaS fees converted at an assumed A$1.50 per US$1, custom maintenance at 15% to 20% of build a year, and hosting at $3k to $6k a year.

| Path (association example, 6,000 members) | Year 1 | Years 2 and 3 | 3 year total |
|---|---|---|---|
| SaaS at about US$3,000 to US$5,400 a year, plus $5k to $15k setup and integration | $9.5k to $23k | $9k to $16k | $18.5k to $39k |
| Partner hosted Moodle: $10k to $40k setup, $3k to $15k a year | $13k to $55k | $6k to $30k | $19k to $85k |
| Custom CPD platform from the worked example | $88k to $120k plus $3k to $6k hosting | $32k to $60k | $123k to $186k |

The custom path costs more on these numbers. It pays back only if course sales are meaningful revenue, if SaaS cannot express the CPD rules your board requires, or if you are building a learning product to sell to other organisations. In that last case, read our [SaaS development cost guide](/guides/saas-development-cost-australia).

## Which content standards and course features add cost?

**SCORM, xAPI and cmi5 decide whether packaged content from authoring tools will play and report correctly, and each one is a scoped line item, not a checkbox.** SCORM 1.2 and SCORM 2004 are what most authoring tools still export, and a solid player with completion and score tracking takes about a week to build and test. xAPI records richer activity statements but needs a learning record store. cmi5 combines xAPI with launch rules similar to SCORM. If your organisation writes all content inside the platform, you may need none of them.

Other features that move the number:

- **Certificates** with verification links, expiry and renewal reminders.
- **CPD tracking** that mirrors a regulator's or board's categories and caps. Our [membership and association software page](/solutions/membership-association-software) covers how CPD rules vary.
- **Assessments** beyond multiple choice: file uploads, marking queues, moderation.
- **Accessibility:** captions, transcripts and keyboard access for every lesson type.

## How do payments and GST work when you sell courses?

**If you are registered for GST, courses sold to Australian customers generally include 10% GST, while the ATO says exports of services from Australia are generally GST free.** The checkout must work out which applies, issue a valid tax invoice and pass the right figures to your accounting system. The rules for overseas buyers have conditions, so confirm the treatment with your accountant before launch.

If you sell courses as a subscription or membership, Australia's new subscription rules apply from 1 July 2027. Our guide to [building a subscription app](/guides/subscription-app-development) covers disclosure and cancellation requirements.

## What do AI features add?

**AI tutoring, quiz drafting and feedback on written answers add build effort and a running cost per learner.** Drafting questions from course material is cheap to add and easy to review. A tutor that answers questions from your content is a retrieval system with its own testing and monitoring, typically several weeks of work. Automated marking of written answers that affect certification needs human review built in. Budget model usage as a monthly running cost that grows with active learners.

## What changes for accredited training?

**Registered training organisations need more than an LMS: they must verify Unique Student Identifiers and report AVETMISS data, which is the job of a student management system.** The USI Registry offers web services that student management systems integrate with. Most RTOs run a specialist student management system alongside an LMS rather than building both.
