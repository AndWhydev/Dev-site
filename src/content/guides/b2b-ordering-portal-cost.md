## What does a B2B ordering portal do that a retail store does not?

**A B2B portal lets known trade customers order on their own terms: their prices, their credit, their usual products.** A retail store sells one price list to anyone. That difference is why a B2B portal costs more than its storefront suggests.

| Capability | Retail store | B2B ordering portal |
|---|---|---|
| Prices | One price list, promotions | Customer or group price lists, contract prices, quantity breaks |
| Payment | Card at checkout | Account terms, credit limits, card, bank transfer or PayTo |
| Buyers | Individuals | Several buyers per company, with approvals and spend limits |
| Ordering | Browse and add to cart | Reorder lists, quick order by SKU, CSV upload, standing orders |
| After the sale | Order confirmation | Invoices, statements, back orders, proof of delivery |
| Catalogue | Everything public | Products and stock visible by customer or region |

This page covers wholesale and trade ordering. If you need a portal for service requests, documents or account management, see our [customer portal guide](/guides/customer-portal-development).

## What do off the shelf B2B options cost per month?

**Dedicated B2B SaaS starts at a few hundred dollars a month, inventory platforms sell B2B as an add on, and Shopify ranges from $42 to $3,700 AUD a month depending on how many catalogues you need.** Prices below are list prices at the time of writing (November 2026).

| Option | Listed price | B2B scope | Watch for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shopify Basic, Grow, Advanced | $42, $114, $431 AUD a month paid yearly | B2B with up to 3 catalogues | Catalogue limit; third party payment fees of 2% to 0.6% if you don't use Shopify Payments |
| Shopify Plus | From $3,700 AUD a month | Unlimited B2B catalogues | Platform cost dominates at small volume |
| B2B Wave | Pro US$350, Scale US$612 a month | Unlimited orders and customers, customer price lists | US dollar billing; ERP connector scope |
| Unleashed B2B eCommerce store | From $119 AUD a month, on top of an Unleashed plan | Ordering store tied to Unleashed stock and pricing | Needs Unleashed as the inventory system |
| Cin7 B2B portal | Included as a Cin7 feature | Self service ordering for customers and sales reps | Needs Cin7; plans start at US$349 a month |

At the RBA's rate of about US$0.69 to the Australian dollar in early November 2026, B2B Wave Scale is roughly $887 AUD a month. Check each vendor's AUD pricing and what its connector to your ERP or accounting system actually syncs.

## What drives the cost of a custom B2B portal?

**Pricing rules and the ERP sync are the two biggest drivers.** The storefront is the smallest part of the work.

| Driver | Lower cost | Higher cost |
|---|---|---|
| Pricing | A few price groups | Contract prices per customer, quantity breaks, promotions by region, rebates |
| ERP or accounting sync | One way order push to Xero or MYOB | Two way sync of products, stock, prices, credit limits, invoices and statements |
| Credit and terms | Show the terms field | Live credit checks, holds over limit, overdue blocks |
| Buyers and approvals | One login per customer | Several buyers, spend limits, manager approval |
| Catalogue | Same catalogue for all | Restricted ranges by customer, region or brand |
| Channels | Web only | Sales rep app, EDI with large retailers |

Each sync and rule needs its own error handling. Our [API integration cost guide](/guides/api-integration-cost-australia) shows how one way and two way syncs differ in effort.

## What do typical builds cost?

**Three bands cover most projects.** These are indicative market ranges, not quotes.

| Build | Typical scope | Indicative range (AUD, ex GST) |
|---|---|---|
| Configured platform portal | Shopify B2B, Cin7 or Unleashed portal set up, with an ERP or accounting connector | $10,000 to $40,000 |
| Custom portal | Customer price lists, credit checks, reorder lists, invoices and statements, Xero or MYOB sync | $50,000 to $120,000 |
| Multi brand trade platform | Several brands or entities, EDI, buyer approvals, a sales rep mobile app | $150,000 to $300,000+ |

The custom band sits within Expeed's published range of $40,000 to $150,000 for a customer portal with account management. Effort converts at an assumed blended agency rate of $1,100 to $1,500 a day; Re:Sourced's July 2026 guide puts senior software contractors in Sydney at $800 to $1,100 a day ex GST, and agencies charge more because their rate includes testing and project management.

## What does it cost over three years? A worked comparison

**For a hypothetical wholesaler with 300 trade accounts and 8 price lists, dedicated B2B SaaS is the cheapest option over three years, and a custom portal costs less than Shopify Plus.** The setup and build figures are assumptions inside the ranges above, at $1,300 a day; it is not a quote.

| Option | Assumptions | 3 year total (AUD, ex GST) |
|---|---|---|
| B2B Wave Scale | About $887 a month × 36, plus $15,000 assumed setup and ERP connector work | $46,932 |
| Custom portal | 55 days × $1,300 = $71,500 build; hosting $3,600 and maintenance at 15% ($10,725) each year | $114,475 |
| Shopify Plus | $3,700 a month × 36, plus $25,000 assumed setup and integration | $158,200 |

Eight price lists rule out the Shopify plans below Plus, which list up to 3 catalogues. The custom portal earns its place if the pricing or credit rules do not fit the SaaS model, if the portal needs to sit inside a wider system the business already owns, or if the business expects to add a rep app or EDI later. Payment fees apply to all three.

## Which option fits which business?

**Match the tool to how your pricing and systems work today, not to the portal you might want in five years.** A rough guide:

- **Shopify below Plus** suits a brand that sells retail online and wants a simple trade price list or two alongside it.
- **Shopify Plus** suits a brand with a large retail store already on Shopify that also needs many B2B catalogues and is comfortable with the platform cost.
- **Dedicated B2B SaaS** suits a wholesaler with many trade accounts, standard ordering and an ERP or accounting system the vendor already connects to.
- **An inventory platform's portal** suits a business that already runs Cin7 or Unleashed and wants orders to land straight in stock.
- **A custom portal** suits a business whose pricing, credit rules or product configuration don't fit any of the above, or that wants the portal inside a wider system it owns.

If two options look close, trial the SaaS first. It is easier to move from a subscription to a custom portal than the other way around, and the trial shows which rules really matter.

## How can you reduce the cost of a first version?

**Launch with reordering, prices and order sync, and leave the rest for phase two.** Most trade customers want to see their price, check stock and repeat last month's order. Credit checks, approvals, rep apps and EDI can follow once you know how customers use the portal. Keep card payments on the provider's hosted checkout, use the ERP as the single source of prices, and resist building custom reports until the standard ones prove inadequate.

## How should a portal handle payments on account, cards and PayTo?

**Offer account terms for established customers and at least one low cost way to pay invoices; do not add card surcharges.** The RBA's FAQs say eftpos, Mastercard and Visa introduced no surcharge rules from 1 October 2026, that card payments between businesses are not exempt unless a network's rules or a law provides an exemption, and that a card payment made on or after that date may not be surcharged even if the invoice was issued earlier.

The same FAQs say the RBA does not regulate surcharges on non card payment methods, and that businesses can offer discounts for particular payment methods, following the ACCC's guidance on price displays. In a portal that usually means:

- account customers pay on terms, by bank transfer or PayTo, with invoices and statements in the portal;
- new or cash customers pay by card at checkout, with card costs built into prices;
- any early payment or payment method discount is shown clearly before checkout.

PayTo lets customers authorise payment agreements from their bank account, which suits recurring trade orders. Our [PayTo integration guide](/guides/payto-integration) covers how it works for businesses.

## How do you roll a portal out to trade customers?

**Treat rollout as its own phase with a named owner, because adoption decides the return.** A typical sequence:

1. **Clean the data first.** Check customer accounts, contacts, price lists and product descriptions in the ERP. The portal will show every error.
2. **Pilot with five to ten friendly accounts** for a few weeks and fix what confuses them.
3. **Onboard in groups,** with a welcome email, a short video and someone to call.
4. **Have sales reps place a first order with each customer** inside the portal.
5. **Keep phone and email ordering open** for a period, and track the share of orders through the portal each month.

## What are the ongoing costs?

**Budget for hosting, maintenance, payment fees and the people who keep product and price data accurate.** For a custom portal, maintenance is roughly 15 to 20% of the build each year, plus hosting and any connector or API fees. For SaaS, the subscription covers the platform, but connectors, apps and extra catalogues often add monthly fees. In both cases the largest hidden cost is data upkeep: wrong prices in a self service portal reach customers immediately.
