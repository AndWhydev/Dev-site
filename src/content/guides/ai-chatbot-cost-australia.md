## How much does an AI chatbot cost? The four tiers compared

**AI chatbot prices fall into four tiers, and the cost jumps between them because each tier takes on more responsibility: from answering questions, to looking things up, to taking actions.** Most small-business cost guides stop at the second or third tier. This one covers all four, from a website chatbot for customer service to a custom AI chatbot built for your own systems.

| Tier | What it does | Upfront cost (AUD, ex GST) | Monthly running cost | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. DIY chatbot tool | Answers questions from your website and FAQs | $0 (self-setup) or $300 to $1,000 for professional configuration | US$19 to US$120 (PSOS) or $0 to $300 (Bumblebee Studio) | Small businesses with common, public questions |
| 2. Configured helpdesk AI | AI agent inside your helpdesk (Intercom, Zendesk, Freshdesk) answering from help articles | $5,000 to $20,000 for setup, content and testing | Seat fees plus per-resolution or per-message charges | Support teams already on that helpdesk |
| 3. Custom chatbot | Your own bot, connected to your knowledge base and a few systems (orders, bookings, CRM) | $10,000 to $30,000 for a simple bot; $30,000 to $80,000 with knowledge base and integrations | About $800 to $10,000 | Higher volumes, specific workflows, data control |
| 4. Enterprise AI agent | Handles authenticated customers or staff, takes actions across systems, escalates with context, full audit trail | $80,000 to $250,000+ | About $9,000 to $35,000 | Large organisations, regulated industries, multiple channels |

Tier 1 figures come from PSOS (US$19 to US$120 a month at small-business volumes) and Bumblebee Studio ($0 to $300 a month DIY, $300 to $1,000 for configuration). Tier 3 and 4 figures come from Team 400's April 2026 guide. The Tier 2 setup range is our estimate of typical configuration work; the platform fees are on each vendor's pricing page.

## Why do Australian chatbot quotes range from $6,500 to $250,000?

**Because the quotes describe different products.** Bumblebee Studio advertises custom builds from $6,500 fixed and enterprise builds at $20,000 to $50,000+. Team 400 puts enterprise AI agents at $80,000 to $250,000+. Both are credible for what they include.

A $6,500 bot answers questions from a fixed body of content on one channel. A $150,000 agent signs a customer in, checks their account, changes a booking, raises a refund under a set limit, hands over to a human with the full conversation, logs every action for audit, and has been tested against hundreds of failure scenarios. The model underneath might be identical. The difference is everything around it.

When you compare quotes, ask each vendor three questions:

1. Which systems will the bot read from, and which can it write to?
2. How is it tested before launch, and how are wrong answers caught afterwards?
3. What happens when it can't answer: what does the human receive?

## What pushes a chatbot into a higher cost tier?

**Integrations, authentication and the right to take actions are what move a chatbot up the tiers.** Team 400 estimates each additional integration at $5,000 to $20,000 depending on the quality of the API.

| Requirement | Why it adds cost |
|---|---|
| Customer login before the bot answers | Identity verification, session handling, and making sure the bot only sees that customer's data |
| Reading from internal systems | An integration per system, plus handling slow or unavailable systems gracefully |
| Writing to systems (bookings, refunds, updates) | Permission rules, confirmation steps, limits, rollback, and an audit log of every action |
| Multiple channels (web, SMS, WhatsApp, voice) | Each channel has its own format, limits and provider fees; voice adds speech recognition and synthesis |
| Large or messy knowledge base | Content clean-up, document ingestion and retrieval tuning; see our [RAG cost guide](/guides/rag-knowledge-base-cost) |
| Regulated advice (financial, health, legal) | Stricter guardrails, disclaimers, human review and much more testing |
| Australian data residency | Hosting and model endpoints in Australian regions, where available, sometimes at a price premium |

## A worked example: helpdesk AI vs a custom chatbot over three years

**At moderate to high volumes, per-resolution pricing and a custom build cross over within two to three years.** This illustration uses published prices at the time of writing and clearly stated assumptions. It is not a quote.

**The scenario:** an Australian retailer's support chatbot resolves 3,000 conversations a month without human help.

**Option A: helpdesk AI agent priced per outcome.** Intercom lists its Fin AI agent from US$0.99 per outcome, on top of per-seat plan fees.

- 3,000 × US$0.99 = US$2,970 a month
- At the Reserve Bank's 25 September 2026 rate of US$0.70 per A$1: US$2,970 ÷ 0.70 ≈ A$4,243 a month
- Per year: A$4,243 × 12 ≈ A$50,900, before seat fees
- Three years: about A$152,700

**Option B: custom chatbot on a model API.** Assume a $60,000 build, and that each conversation sends about 6,000 input tokens (instructions, retrieved help content and history across several turns) and receives about 800 output tokens. On a mid-tier model priced at US$2 per million input tokens and US$10 per million output tokens:

- Input: 3,000 × 6,000 = 18 million tokens × US$2 = US$36
- Output: 3,000 × 800 = 2.4 million tokens × US$10 = US$24
- Model cost: US$60 a month ≈ A$86
- Hosting, logging and monitoring: about A$500 a month
- Maintenance and content updates: 15% of the build a year = A$9,000
- Running cost per year: (A$86 + A$500) × 12 + A$9,000 ≈ A$16,000
- Three years: A$60,000 + (3 × A$16,000) = about A$108,000

**What the numbers mean.** Option A is cheaper in year one (A$50,900 against A$76,000) and needs no build. Option B is about A$45,000 cheaper over three years at this volume, and the gap widens as volume grows. At 500 resolutions a month, Option A costs about A$8,500 a year and would stay cheaper for a long time.

The comparison isn't only about money. Helpdesk AI comes with a mature inbox, reporting and handover built in, which a custom build must recreate or integrate with. A custom build gives you control over the model, the data flow, where data is processed, and what the bot can do in your own systems.

## How much does an AI chatbot cost per month?

**Monthly running costs range from under $100 for a DIY tool to over $15,000 for a busy enterprise agent: budget for model usage, hosting, monitoring, content upkeep and someone reviewing conversations.** Team 400's April 2026 figures give a useful spread by tier:

| Monthly cost (AUD) | Simple FAQ bot | Integrated chatbot | Enterprise agent |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI model usage | $200 to $1,000 | $500 to $3,000 | $2,000 to $15,000 |
| Hosting and infrastructure | $100 to $300 | $300 to $1,000 | $1,000 to $5,000 |
| Monitoring and maintenance | $500 to $1,500 | $1,500 to $4,000 | $4,000 to $10,000 |
| Content updates | As needed | $1,000 to $2,000 | $2,000 to $5,000 |

These model usage figures are higher than the token arithmetic above suggests, which is normal: real systems send longer context, retry, run safety checks and use premium models for harder questions. Our [LLM running costs guide](/guides/llm-running-costs) explains how to estimate and control that bill.

Hidden costs that are not usually in the quote: GST at 10%, messaging channel fees (SMS and WhatsApp are charged per message by the provider), helpdesk seat licences, and your team's time writing and approving content.

## Is an AI chatbot worth the cost?

**An AI chatbot is worth the cost when it resolves a meaningful volume of repetitive questions that would otherwise take staff time, without frustrating the customers it can't help.** It is not worth it when enquiries are few, varied or emotionally charged, or when the underlying content is out of date.

A quick way to test it: count the conversations a bot could resolve on its own each month, multiply by the staff time each one takes, and compare that with the monthly cost for the right tier in the table above. At 3,000 resolutions a month, the worked example shows running costs between about A$1,300 and A$4,250 a month depending on the option. At 50 enquiries a month, even a DIY tool may not pay for the time spent maintaining it. Remember that a chatbot rarely removes the need for people; it changes what they spend their time on.

## How can you keep chatbot costs down?

**Match the tier to the job, and invest in content before you invest in complexity.**

1. **Start with the questions you actually get.** Export three months of enquiries and group them. The top 20 question types often cover most volume.
2. **Fix the knowledge base first.** Bumblebee Studio and PSOS both make the point that the bot is only as good as the answers you give it.
3. **Launch read-only.** Let the bot answer and look things up before it's allowed to change anything.
4. **Use a cheaper model for simple turns** and a stronger model only when needed.
5. **Design a good handover.** A clean escalation to a human is cheaper than a bot that tries too hard and upsets customers.
6. **Review conversations weekly** for the first few months. Wrong answers found early are cheap to fix.
