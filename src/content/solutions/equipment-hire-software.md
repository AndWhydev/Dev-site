## Why isn't the booking calendar the hard part?

**Because every hire platform can show what is out and what is free. Few of them protect you when a customer goes broke holding your excavator, or prove you checked the operator's licence.** A hire company's real exposure sits in four places: the legal status of long hires, the safety of the plant you supply, the competence of the people who use it, and the terms you rely on when it comes back damaged.

## When does a long hire have to go on the PPSR?

**When it becomes a PPS lease: a lease or bailment by a business regularly engaged in hiring, for more than two years, or renewable beyond two years, or indefinite once the customer has held the goods for two years.** The PPSR explains that the definition changed in 2015 and again in 2017. For hires starting on or after 20 May 2017, the minimum moved from more than one year to more than two years. Pooling arrangements, such as pallets passing between users, are not PPS leases.

Registration matters most when a customer becomes insolvent. The PPSR says a correct registration protects you and gives you extra rights in the property, especially in insolvency.

**A worked example, from the PPSR's own case study.** A hire company's standard terms allow open ended hire: a 48 hour minimum, fortnightly invoicing for continued use, and a week's notice to return. A transport customer takes a heavy haulage truck for a road project expected to last about two and a half years, and goes into liquidation just after two years. The liquidator searches the PPSR and finds nothing against the truck's serial number or the customer's ACN. Because the indefinite hire had run past two years, it was a PPS lease, the unregistered interest was ineffective against the liquidator, and the hire company became an unsecured creditor. The PPSR adds that the result would be the same for a six month fixed hire that rolled on for another 18 months.

The software lesson is that a hire's legal status depends on how long it actually runs, not on what the booking said. A useful PPSR module:

1. Reads every open hire from your platform daily, with asset serial number and customer ACN or ABN.
2. Flags hires whose terms allow more than two years, and open ended hires approaching the threshold.
3. Prepares the registration with the right collateral class and serial number for review.
4. Records the registration number, expiry and verification statement against the hire.
5. Raises a task when a hire ends, so the registration is ended too.

Some hire businesses register open ended hires from the start rather than wait for the threshold. Set the trigger with your lawyer.

## What does a hire company owe on plant safety?

**A business that hires out plant has duties both as a supplier and as a person with management or control of plant, so it must make sure, so far as reasonably practicable, that the plant is safe and maintained and that safety information goes with it.** Safe Work Australia's model Code of Practice on plant says the supplier is in most cases responsible for inspecting and maintaining hired plant, and that on extended hires the parties may agree who maintains it on site, with maintenance records handed back at the end.

Some plant also needs registration. The Code lists items that must be item registered before they are commissioned for use, including tower cranes, mobile cranes with a rated capacity over 10 tonnes, concrete placing booms, lifts, and certain boilers and pressure vessels. A hire fleet system should hold each asset's registration, inspection history, logbook and service schedule, and block an asset from going out when an inspection is overdue.

## How do you check operator licences at hire time?

**By recording the licence class and number for plant that needs one, and checking it against the state register where one exists.** SafeWork NSW says there are 29 classes of high risk work licence, licences expire after 5 years, and holder details can be checked on the Verify NSW public register.

| Plant | NSW licence position |
|---|---|
| Forklift truck | Forklift truck class (LF) |
| Order picking forklift | Order picking class (LO) |
| Boom type elevating work platform | Licence class WP where the boom is 11 metres or more |
| Telehandler with forks or bucket | No high risk work licence, but the business must ensure operators are trained |
| Telehandler with a personnel box and a boom of 11 metres or more | WP class |

The legal duty to use licensed operators sits with the business running the work, but capturing licence details at the counter or in an online checkout costs little and shows you took care. Other states issue licences under the same national classes with their own registers.

## How do you keep damage waivers and deposits fair?

**Put the terms in plain words before the hire, make waivers and excess clear, and record the condition of every item going out and coming back.** Hire terms are standard form contracts, and since 9 November 2023 unfair terms in them are banned with penalties. The ACCC's examples of terms that may be unfair include terms that penalise only one party for breaching or ending a contract.

The ACCC's advice to car hire customers shows what a fair process looks like: inspect with a staff member, photograph existing damage, record it on the contract, and get written agreement on any damage found at return. A hire app that captures timestamped photos at dispatch and return, linked to the contract, settles most damage disputes before they start.

## Is telematics worth integrating?

**For high value plant, yes: utilisation, hours and location data drive service schedules, billing accuracy and buying decisions.** Many machinery makers expose telematics through APIs, and ISO/TS 15143-3 defines a standard format for earthmoving and road construction machinery telematics data. A dashboard that joins machine hours with hire records shows which assets earn their keep, which are due for service by hours rather than by date, and which hires ran over.
