## Notes

- Improve existing rows take the hub cluster of the page that answers them; new rows take the cluster they were curated under.
- "Grounded" means the question's normalised words closely match (IDF weighted Jaccard 0.6 or more) a Google autocomplete (en-AU) suggestion in docs-internal/keywords. It shows real search phrasing exists, not search volume.
- Coverage for curated rows was matched automatically against page titles, H1s, H2s and FAQs, then corrected by hand in scripts/data/question-bank-coverage-overrides.json after spot checks.
- Per row provenance (source, grounding match, coverage match, merged duplicates, other pages answering the same question) is in reports/question-bank-provenance.csv.
- The industry-software hub holds every solution and industry page FAQ (1209 rows); it will need sub-hubs (health, trades and construction, finance, property, logistics, education, hospitality) rather than one list.
